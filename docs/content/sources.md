## N5 expansion

- **Selection:** Onihayo-authored N5-oriented inventory, source ID `onihayo.n5.v1`,
  decided in [ADR 0013](../decisions/0013-n5-vocabulary-selection.md). Independently
  curated selection, coverage rationales and lesson ordering are CC BY-SA 4.0,
  attributed to "Onihayo contributors" (ADR 0006). The manifest
  `src/lib/content/vocabulary/selection.json` lists 633 entries in 15 coverage
  categories, each with a rationale; the dataset is imported from it.
- **Lexical source:** reuse the pinned JMdict Japanese/English import above, with
  EDRDG credit and CC BY-SA 4.0 on adaptations. Publisher terms rechecked on
  2026-10-15. Preserve the existing licence, NOTICE and displayed attribution.
  No community word list is imported or needs attribution by this decision.
- **Meaning of N5:** a curriculum target, not an official exhaustive list or a
  guarantee of exam coverage. The [JLPT FAQ](https://jlpt.jp/sp/e/faq/index.html)
  and [level summary](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/about/levelsummary.html) inform the
  scope only; their text and exam questions are not copied as learning content.

## N5 kanji

- **Selection:** Onihayo-authored N5-oriented kanji selection, source ID
  `onihayo.n5.kanji.v1`, decided in [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-n5-kanji-selection-and-sources.md).
  Membership, rationales, example words and teaching order are CC BY-SA 4.0, attributed
  to "Onihayo contributors". Candidates come from KANJIDIC2's pre-2010 level 4 (103 kanji
  in the 2026-10-26 file); every selected kanji needs a rationale and an `onihayo.n5.v1`
  example word. The manifest is committed with the import.
- **Kanji data:** KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG), CC BY-SA 4.0, limited to the character, code point,
  grade, stroke count, on/kun readings, and English meanings. No KANJIDIC2 data is
  committed yet.
- **Stroke data:** KanjiVG (Ulrich Apel), CC BY-SA 3.0, from a pinned release. No KanjiVG
  data is committed yet.

| WaniKani, Bunpro, Nihondex, and other commercial learning apps                 | Proprietary content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

| Guides under NonCommercial licences (e.g. Tae Kim's Guide to Japanese Grammar) | NC terms are incompatible with free hosting choices ([ADR 0006](../decisions/0006-code-and-content-licensing.md))                                                                                                                                        |
| Any website without an explicit open licence                                   | Visible is not licensed; scraping is not allowed                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
